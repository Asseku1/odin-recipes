Scrambled Eggs

Delicious and easy to make! Scrambled eggs are a classic breakfast dish that's quick to prepare and always satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them together.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
  4. Cook until the edges are set, then gently stir to create fluffy curds.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
