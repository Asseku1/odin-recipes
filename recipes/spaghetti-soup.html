Spaghetti Soup

This is a simple recipe for delicious spaghetti soup! Spaghetti soup is a hearty and satisfying meal that's perfect for a quick weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook spaghetti according to package directions.
  2. In a large pot, sauté the onion and garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the diced tomatoes and basil.
  4. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Combine the cooked spaghetti with the sauce.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
