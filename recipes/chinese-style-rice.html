Chinese Style Rice

This is a simple recipe for Chinese style rice. The Chinese style rice is a delicious and easy-to-make dish that combines cooked rice with vegetables and eggs.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan or wok over medium heat.
  2. Add the mixed vegetables and sauté until they are tender.
  3. Push the vegetables to one side of the pan and pour the beaten eggs into the other side. Scramble the eggs until they are fully cooked.
  4. Add the cooked rice to the pan and mix everything together.
  5. Pour the soy sauce over the rice and stir well to combine.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the rice is heated through.
